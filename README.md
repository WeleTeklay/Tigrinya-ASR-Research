# Tigrinya ASR Research

Research on low-resource Tigrinya Automatic Speech Recognition (ASR) systems conducted during my Machine Learning Research Internship at Lesan.AI.

## Research Overview

This repository contains research materials, experiments, reports, and presentation resources related to the evaluation of low-resource ASR systems for Tigrinya.

## Research Contributions

- Curated and processed 10,000+ Tigrinya speech utterances
- Conducted ASR benchmarking and evaluation
- Performed linguistic and acoustic error analysis
- Worked with Whisper-based ASR systems
- Contributed to establishing baseline ASR benchmarks for Tigrinya
